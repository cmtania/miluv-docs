Getting started

How do I connect with my partner?

You both install MiLuv and create an account. One of you opens Connect your partner and shares the link code; the other enters it. Once you’re linked, the widget starts showing the distance between you.

Does my partner need an iPhone too?

Yes — you both need the MiLuv app and an account of your own. MiLuv links exactly two people.

Can I link with more than one person?

No. MiLuv connects one pair at a time. To link with someone else, unlink your current partner first (Settings › Unlink partner).

Can we be in different countries or time zones?

Yes. Distance is distance — borders and time zones don’t matter. The widget shows the straight-line distance between you wherever you both are.

Add the widget to your Home Screen

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap + in the top corner, search for MiLuv, and add the small or medium size.

How do I remove the widget?

Touch and hold the widget, then tap Remove Widget.

Location & the widget

The widget shows an old time or isn’t updating

Open MiLuv once to refresh it. Then check that location access is set to Always in iOS Settings › MiLuv › Location, and that both you and your partner have a connection. The widget updates in the background, but only if the app is allowed to.

Why does MiLuv ask for “Always” location?

The widget and the proximity notifications need to keep working when the app is closed, and that requires background location. MiLuv still works with While Using — it just only updates while the app is open.

How often does the distance update, and will it drain my battery?

In the background, roughly every 15 minutes — or sooner once you’ve moved about 150 m. MiLuv uses “balanced” accuracy (which leans on Wi-Fi and cell rather than constant GPS), so it’s light on battery. It is not a live tracker.

How accurate is the distance?

It’s approximate — usually within about 100 m. That’s plenty for “how far apart are we,” and it’s deliberately not precise enough to pinpoint a place.

What if I lose signal or turn my phone off?

The distance simply stops updating. Your partner keeps seeing the last known distance and the time it was measured, and it catches up once you’re back online.

Can my partner see where I am?

No. Your partner only ever sees the distance between you and a timestamp — never your coordinates, never a map, never an address. Only your latest position is stored, and it’s overwritten on every update. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Notifications

I’m not getting the “getting closer” notifications

Make sure notifications are enabled in iOS Settings › MiLuv. They only fire as the distance between you shrinks past about 1 km, then 500 m, then when you’ve arrived — and both partners’ locations need to be updating for the distance to change.

I didn’t get the “arrived” one

Each notification fires once, as the distance drops through its threshold. If you were already inside that range, or the distance never decreased past it, there’s nothing to announce. Each one re-arms after you move apart again.

Do they come through on silent or a Focus?

MiLuv’s notifications follow whatever you’ve set for it in iOS Settings › Notifications and in your Focus settings, the same as any other app.

Names & display

How do I change my name, or how my partner’s name shows?

Settings. Your name is what your partner sees. Nickname for your partner is private to you — set it to change what you see, or leave it blank to show their own name.

The widget says “Open to connect”

You’re not linked with anyone yet. Open MiLuv and connect with your partner, and the widget will start showing the distance.

Managing your account

How do I unlink or start over?

Settings › Unlink partner. You can link again with a new code afterwards.

What happens if my partner deletes their account or unlinks?

Your link ends and the widget goes back to the “not connected” state. Your own account and data are untouched, and you can link with someone else whenever you like.

Where is my data stored?

On Supabase, our hosted database provider. MiLuv keeps your email, your single most-recent location (overwritten every update — no history), and your pairing. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your account, your location, and your pairing — there’s no undo.

Is MiLuv free?

It’s free at launch. It may later become a small one-time purchase — if you download it while it’s free, it stays free for you.
