MiLuv · for two

Know how far

MiLuv is a quiet Home Screen widget for two people who live apart. It shows the distance between you, gives you a heads-up as it shrinks, and stays out of the way the rest of the time.

  • Distance and a timestamp — never a map
  • A gentle heads-up as you get closer
  • Send a quick “I miss you!” nudge
  • Linked to one partner, always

What it does

Just the distance. Nothing else to manage.

No feed, no chat, no map to check obsessively. MiLuv does one thing quietly, all day, from your Home Screen.

Live distance, on your Home Screen

Add the MiLuv widget and watch the number between you update itself, all day, without opening the app.

A heads-up as you get closer

A gentle notification at about 1 km, another at 500 m, and one the moment you’ve arrived.

Private by design

Your partner only ever sees a distance and a timestamp — never a pin, a map, or your exact location.

Send a nudge

Pick “I miss you!”, “I love you!”, or “I can’t wait to see you!” and tap Nudge It — it lands on their phone as a notification.

Just the two of you

MiLuv links one pair at a time. No groups, no discovery feed — only the person you connect with.

Getting started

Set up in about a minute

Both of you install the app; after that, MiLuv runs quietly in the background.

1

Create your account

Install MiLuv and each of you signs up with your own email.

2

Share your link code

One of you opens Connect Your Partner and shares the code; the other enters it.

3

Add the widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, add MiLuv, and watch the distance update on its own.

No coordinates. No history. No map.

MiLuv keeps only your single most recent location, overwritten on every update — there’s no trail to look back through. Your partner sees the distance between you and when it was last measured, and that’s the whole picture.
